# Project Title

[Project title]'s purpose is to assist the user in understanding Singapore's policies, 
aiding user's decision making process.

## How does [Project Title] work?

[Project Title] was created as part of the SimplifyNext Agentic AI Hackathon 2026.

Understanding and following developments in policy is important for individual Singaporeans as policy impacts their the day to day living.
Unfortunately, policies are often long, dry and complex, requiring significant time and effort to understand.
Additionally, it is difficult to follow proposed changes to policy as these discussions are often hidden under hours of parliamentary debate. 
This results in Singaporeans often being more politically apathetic, being unprepared for policy change that they could have known about if they were more in tune with news and debates.

[Project Title] aims to resolve this issue through two main functions; a policy summariser and a debate summariser.

The policy summariser: Summarises existing policy and presents it in a "news-article" like way, pulling and citing information from both official and unofficial sources. 

The debate summariser: Summarises the content of a parliamentary debate session.

Users may consult an AI helper, allowing them to narrow down relevant information which further aids in their decision making.
